Identify the chapter claim. Read the corresponding claim-to-source note and BibTeX entry. Open the primary source and record the section or page supporting the claim. Separate a platform statement from a general research result. Report unsupported details as gaps.

Read the [project role definition](../../agents/researcher.md) for inputs, scope and output ownership. For a scoped verification, give the coordinator the evidence and assessment as a handoff rather than overwriting the original run record.
